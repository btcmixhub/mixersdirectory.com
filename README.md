How we rate services

No paid rankings. Sponsored banners are always labeled and never change a score.

- Privacy model
  
CoinJoin, custodial pool or zero-knowledge contracts. What the operator can see, and what it keeps.

- Track record
  
Years online, incident history, how disputes were handled and whether letters of guarantee are signed.

- Fees and limits
  
Percentage and fixed fees, minimum and maximum amounts, and whether the fee is randomized.

- Control and access
  
Custom delays, multiple outputs, Tor mirrors, no-JavaScript support and clear instructions.

What is the best bitcoin mixer?

There is no single winner for everyone; the best BTC mixer for you depends on the amount and how much you trust the operator. Among bitcoin-only services, CryptoMixer, Whir and YoMix score highest in our 2026 ranking: CryptoMixer for large amounts thanks to its reserve pool, Whir for small amounts with CoinJoin, and YoMix for high limits. Choose by amount, fee and mixing model, and check the [comparison table](https://mixersdirectory.com/).
